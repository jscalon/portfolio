Complete redesign of the official website of **Botinfy**, a software development company.

## The problem

The previous website was **outdated and conveyed little professionalism**. For a company
whose business is *building software*, the official site is a critical showcase: visitors
expect a site that matches the quality of the service offered, and the old one did not
inspire that confidence.

## The solution

A **modern, professional and responsive** website that reflects the company's technical
level and better captures the attention of its audience, with care put into both the
desktop and mobile experience.

## My role

I took part in the design and led the frontend development of the site.

- **Design (UI/UX):** desktop and mobile versions in Figma.
- **Frontend:** Next.js, TypeScript and Tailwind CSS.

## Result

The site is **deployed and in production**, publicly available at
[botinfy.com](https://botinfy.com).
